HERMES • PRIVACY

Privacy policy

This draft describes Gaurav Chaddah’s private Hermes integration. It is not a privacy policy for all Hermes Agent installations, Harman customers or Friends of Paws donors.

1. Operator and purpose

Gaurav Chaddah operates the integration for personal productivity and authorised business and animal-welfare work. Google data is accessed to carry out requested tasks such as finding messages, generating briefings, retrieving files and preparing reports.

2. Google data accessed

Depending on the authorised account and task, data may include email headers, senders and recipients, message bodies, attachments, labels, Drive file metadata and file contents. OAuth access and refresh tokens allow approved access without storing the Google account password.

  • GSC: Gmail read-only and Drive read-only.
  • FOPF: Gmail read-only, send and modify; full Drive access.

The FOPF scopes can technically support changes and deletions. Approval rules are workflow safeguards, not a claim that Google technically prohibits those actions. The current authorisation does not include Google Calendar.

3. Use of information

Information is used to provide the requested assistant functions, maintain task continuity and produce user-directed outputs. Daily briefings are read-only. Messages and files are not intentionally made public through this informational site.

4. Processing and sharing

The integration runs on managed Hermes hosting. Selected task content, instructions and conversation context may be transmitted to the configured AI provider, currently OpenAI. Results and user-submitted content may pass through Telegram. Google supplies the connected APIs. Hosting, AI and messaging providers process data under their applicable terms and policies; processing is not guaranteed to remain on one device or in one country.

Human access is limited by operational need, permissions and provider policies. This draft does not assert that all infrastructure staff access is technically impossible. GitHub Pages, if used, hosts only these public informational files; it must not host credentials, mailbox contents, private reports or session histories. Hosting providers may process visitor IP addresses and request logs. These static pages add no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or forms.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

The deployment stores account tokens and may retain session histories, downloaded attachments, cached content and generated reports on its server. Starting a new chat or revoking Google access does not automatically delete those existing copies. No fixed automatic deletion period has been established for this deployment.

Contact the operator to request review or deletion of retained data. The operator must identify relevant copies, backups and provider-held records and explain any retention limits; an immediate or universal deletion guarantee is not made. Routine preservation of business and welfare records is subject to the user’s instructions and applicable obligations.

6. Security

Google authorisation uses OAuth. Named account connections are kept separate, and credential access is restricted within the deployment. No system is risk-free; this document does not claim independently audited security, universal encryption at rest or a completed provider compliance assessment.

7. Limited Use and AI processing

Hermes’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data must not be sold, used for advertising, or used to train or improve generalised AI/ML models.

Before publication and continued processing, the operator must verify that the configured AI provider account and data controls satisfy these restrictions. The provider account’s training and retention settings have not been independently verified in this draft; no blanket provider non-training guarantee is asserted.

8. Revoke access or contact us

You can remove the app’s access in Google Account → Third-party connections. This prevents further access through that authorisation but does not erase previously stored copies. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact gaurav.chaddah@gmail.com.

9. Changes

Material changes to permissions, providers or data practices require a policy review. An effective date will be added when the operator approves the final policy for publication.
