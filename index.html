Hermes helps Gaurav Chaddah review important messages, find documents and prepare useful updates across personal and authorised organisation workflows.

What this integration does

This privately operated integration connects approved Google accounts to an AI assistant. It supports email summaries, document retrieval, daily briefings and preparation of Friends of Paws Foundation reports. It is not a public sign-up service.

Separate accounts, separate permissions

Personal Google account — GSC

Read-only Gmail and Google Drive access supports message review and document retrieval. This account does not grant email sending or Drive modification permissions.

Friends of Paws Foundation — FOPF

Gmail read, send and modify permissions, plus full Google Drive access, support authorised NGO correspondence and report management. These scopes are technically broader than read-only access. Sending, uploading, sharing, replacing or deleting content requires specific user approval under the operator’s workflow.

Your approval remains central

Daily briefings operate read-only. Google Calendar is not included in the current Google authorisation. Other calendar and business-service connections are separate. Public information pages do not provide access to connected accounts.

How data is handled

Relevant content may be processed by the configured AI provider and delivered through Telegram. This is not a local-only system. Read the privacy policy for access, storage, sharing and revocation details.
